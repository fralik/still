Still is a simple weight journal inspired by DroidWeight.

Record daily measurements, review your history, and follow your progress without an account.

Features:
* Weight entries in kilograms or pounds
* Optional body fat, waist measurements, and notes
* Weight charts, seven-day averages, and period statistics
* Optional goal weight and BMI reference
* Daily reminders
* System, light, and dark themes
* Full backup and restore, including settings
* CSV import/export and DroidWeight import

Each date has one entry. Charts show weight; other measurements appear in entry details.

Full backups replace the destination journal and settings. CSV imports add missing dates without overwriting existing entries. Exported files are not encrypted.

Still works offline, with no ads, analytics, or internet permission. Cloud backup and multi-device synchronization are not supported.
